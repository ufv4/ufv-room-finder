UFV Room Finder

View campus floor plans, find available rooms, and see scheduled room bookings.

Last updated: —

This project provides a simple way to view UFV campus maps/floor plans, find available rooms, and see scheduled room bookings. It uses data from UFV's Room Bookings Portal and campus maps/floor plans from UFV's Maps and Floor Plans page. While efforts are made to ensure data used is accurate and up to date, information may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect, and may not reflect last-minute changes, cancellations, closures, or unscheduled use. This is an unofficial, independently created tool and is not an official UFV service.

UFV Room Bookings page: https://www.ufv.ca/registrar/schedule-rooms/room-bookings/
